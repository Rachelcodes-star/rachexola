From earth to everyday life

Why minerals matter

Minerals and mineral-based materials are part of the infrastructure and products people use every day.

  • Technology: copper wiring, tin solder, tantalum components.
  • Construction: steel, gypsum boards, cement ingredients, aggregates.
  • Transport: metals and alloys in vehicles, railways, and aircraft.
  • Energy: materials for power networks, batteries, and energy systems.
  • Manufacturing: inputs for glass, ceramics, tools, and machinery.

Responsible sourcing and recycling can reduce environmental and social impacts.

Local perspective

Rwanda's mineral resources

Rwanda's mining sector is especially known for the 3Ts—tin, tantalum, and tungsten—as well as gold and other mineral resources. Mining contributes to livelihoods, exports, and industrial development.
